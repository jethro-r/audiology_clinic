**04 — DO THESE FIRST** 

## **Immediate interventions** 

Zero organic keywords with a live, functioning website almost always traces to one of these. Work down this list before anything else — content and links won't help if the site isn't being crawled and read properly. 

##### **[THIS WEEK]  Confirm indexing in Google Search Console** 

Verify the property, check the Pages report for "not indexed" pages, submit an XML sitemap, and request indexing on the homepage, Services and About pages manually. Zero ranked keywords after this long online usually means Search Console has never been set up. 

##### **[THIS WEEK]  Check robots.txt and meta robots tags** 

Confirm nothing is set to noindex or disallowed — a common leftover from a staging build that never got switched off for launch. 

##### **[THIS WEEK]  Give every page a unique title tag and meta description** 

Include "Hamilton" / "Frankton" and the specific service on every service page. Generic or duplicate titles across pages are the next most common reason a small site earns zero rankings even once indexed. 

##### **[THIS WEEK]  Publish Paul's bio on the About/Team page** 

Right now the only trace of Paul on the site is a LinkedIn link — no name context, credentials or photo on-page. This is free trust-building for patients, and the single highest-leverage change for both Google's people-first ranking signals and for being cited by AI answer engines. 

##### **[NEXT 2 WEEKS]  Add LocalBusiness + Physician schema markup** 

Structured data for the clinic (name, address, phone, hours, services) and for Paul as a credentialed practitioner. Cheap to add, and it's what both Google and AI systems use to confirm you're a real, specific, local provider. 

##### **[NEXT 2 WEEKS]  List on Healthpoint.co.nz** 

Every competitor in this report — including the two closest independent comparables — is listed here. It's the standard clinical directory in NZ and the single most valuable citation you don't have yet. 

##### **[ONGOING / LOW PRIORITY]  Keep an eye on the two low-quality inbound links** 

newsblogsports.site and robuta.com aren't causing harm at this size — don't spend time disavowing yet. Revisit only if more low-quality links show up as the profile grows.

###### **07 — AI VISIBILITY** 

## **Getting into AI Overview** 

The SERP data draws a clean line: hyper-local "near me"-type searches (hearing aids hamilton, audiologist hamilton, hearing test hamilton) do NOT trigger Google's AI Overview — those are won through the local map pack, where Veritas already leads. National informational searches (hearing aids nz, signs of hearing loss, best hearing aids nz, tinnitus treatment nz) DO trigger AI Overview — that's the door in. 

- **Answer the actual question in the first 40–60 words.** AI Overview extracts direct answers — write the answer to "What are the early signs of hearing loss?" as the first paragraph under that exact heading. 

- **Byline everything with Paul's name and NZAS credentials.** AI systems favour content with clear, identifiable, first-hand expertise over anonymous marketing copy — currently missing site-wide. 

- **Add FAQPage and Article/MedicalWebPage schema** to every guide — it's how both Google's classic rich results and AI systems parse a direct Q&A structure. 

- **Write like an independent clinician, not a retailer.** Every top-ranking competitor page on pricing and "best hearing aids" is trying to sell its own stock. Veritas's "no sales pressure" positioning is a genuine content advantage here. 

- **Get corroborated elsewhere.** A mention or citation on audiology.org.nz, Healthpoint or a Health NZ page adds cross-source confirmation, weighted heavily by both classic SEO and AI answer engines. 

**First three pages to build for this:** "How much do hearing aids really cost in NZ" (vol 90, diff 1), "Early signs of hearing loss" (vol 80, diff 3), "Best hearing aids in NZ, compared" (vol 400, diff 0) — all AI-Overviewactive, all low difficulty, all currently owned by sales-driven competitors.

**10 — LONG GAME** 

## **The moat** 

What makes any of this defensible once a national chain notices — the point of a moat is that it's structurally hard for a competitor to copy, not just currently missing from their site. 

##### **[POSITIONING]  Independence is a real, rare category** 

Bay Audiology, Triton, Audika, Bloom and Specsavers are all tied to hearing-aid manufacturers or retail groups. They cannot credibly claim "no sales pressure, evidence-based advice" without undermining their own commercial model — Veritas can own that phrase outright. 

##### **[CONTENT]  A compounding, citable content hub** 

Resonate Health's "A-Z" glossary proves the model: 15–20 genuinely useful, well-linked explainer pages become a durable traffic asset that keeps earning citations years after publication. 

##### **[RELATIONSHIPS]  A hyperlocal referral network** 

GPs, optometrists, ACC, Veteran Affairs and retirement villages in Frankton/Hamilton are slow for a national chain to replicate at this granularity — each relationship is also a backlink and a review source. 

##### **[LOCAL SEARCH]  An already-won, defensible local pack position** 

Local pack rankings are sticky once established — regular reviews, photos and GBP posts protect the "audiologist hamilton" position Veritas already holds. 

##### **[EXPERIENCE]  Direct clinician access** 

"Book directly with your audiologist, not a dispenser" is an experience the chains are structurally built not to offer — a genuine, hard-to-copy differentiator.
